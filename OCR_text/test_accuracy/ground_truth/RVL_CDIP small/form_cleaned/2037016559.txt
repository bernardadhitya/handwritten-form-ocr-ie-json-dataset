APPLICABLE TO:  Ulisite/waste treatment

SUBJECT
Taking dips on sludge thickener

REQUIRED PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
Rubber gloves

REQUIRED TOOLS & EQUIPMENT
dip tube, stopper

APPROVAL
A. Bledge		M. Mindill

I Potential Hazards
a. Lacerations, contusions, and/or abrasions could result due to striking surrounding structures, equipment or supplies.
b. Slips and falls could result in death by drowning or serious injury due to wet surfaces.
c. Bodily injury could result from slips and falls from elevated surfaces (ascending/descending).
d. Muscle strain could result from improper bending to take dips.

II Job Steps
a. Sequence of Steps
1. Descend on dip platform.
2. Dip tank by bending at knees, keeping back straight.
3. Insert dip tube between weir and scuffle plate.
4. After water is in the top of dip tube, insert stopper.
5. Stand up and read sludge level in tube.
6. After all dips have been taken, store dip tube in proper storage area.
