Please be advised that on June 23, 1995 I called your 1-800 # to get an address to write this letter. My name, address, age and phone # was taken by the representative that answered the phone.

I have smoked Doral 100's bull flavor for over three years.

During the last month I have experienced congestion in the chest, sore throat and nausea. I saw a doctor at University Medical here in Sarasota, FL and he told me my entire throat was burnt and my vocal chords were swollen.

I had a raspy voice and very bad cough for almost three weeks.

I literally became nauseous after each cigarette. I since have switched brands (Capri ultra light) and the problem is reversing itself.

I am only sending this letter to go on record as to how ill I became. If it was a bad batch, I don't know.
