QR, Jan 16-Mar 8!  

All work this quarter has related to the analysis of sidestream tar, nicotine, CO and CO2. Initially the Bogwaldt sidestream apparatus was set up. Modifications made to remove dead column and then the system evaluated for tar & nic determinations. The Harris stirred trap is used for smoke collection with isopropanol maintained at 0-1°C as the trapping liquid. A single trap is used to collect smoke.  

Carryover into a second stirred trap was found to be only 3.5% for tar and 3.2% for nicotine. Values could be corrected for this loss of desired.  

Five cigarettes are smoked per sample. The isopropanol is then divided, the tar analyzed by a spectrophotometric procedure and the nicotine by initial GC.  

For the sidestream collection, a pump is used to pull 1250 cc/min of air across the fire cone of each cigarette. It was felt the easiest way to analyze CO, CO2 would be to collect this air and analyze it using the IR analyzer. The total volume of air that is pumped during the cigarette smoking is collected in a sealed bag and analyzed by the same as mainstream CO, CO2. Tests were made collecting the air after passing through a stirred trap.
