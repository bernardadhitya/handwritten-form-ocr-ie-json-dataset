Dear Camel Employer & Employee,

How could you use such a gimmick to trick teenagers and younger, to use your matches? "Do not distribute these matches or tobacco products to minors." simply motivates them to obtain them and use the cigarette also. (Of course you know this.)

I found several boxes of matches & camel cigarette hidden in my son's room. I am a concerned parent for today's teenagers. I told my son if I found out that he was smoking, his permit would return to me until he was 18 even if this meant walking to work or wherever. I also told him that I gave him life and he was taking it away.

If you are concerned for our teenagers today, you will stop using the "Camel" as a teenager and especially in a "rock band" scene.

I cover every teenager with this kind of Jesus, who has ever seen this ad of yours and pray they will reject cigarettes.

A concerned parent!
Bruder H. Smith
