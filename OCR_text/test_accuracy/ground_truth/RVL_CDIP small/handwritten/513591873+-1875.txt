RECEIVED
JUL 14 1988

Dear Mr. Long,

I receive and enjoy your Choice Magazine. That is how I happen to have your name.

I am writing to you about the Millionaire Cash Quiz that R.J. Reynolds is conducting.

I have a large family, 10 of whom are Camel smokers.

I entered your contest and believe me even with that many smokers amassing 607 tickets for May was not easy. But evidently at least 111 other people amassed more than that. This is hard for me to believe, because to answer some of the questions took a lot of research. Anyway I am enclosing a letter that I am returning to the Quiz Dept.

Thank you for listening
Mrs. Dixie Cole
