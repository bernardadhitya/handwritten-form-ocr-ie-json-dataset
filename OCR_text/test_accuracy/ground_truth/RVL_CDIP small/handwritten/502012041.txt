7 December 1976

R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Company  
Winston-Salem N.C. 27102

Dear Sirs,  
I have been a faithful NOW smoker since last February (76). Previously I smoked WINSTON for years. I have even converted several of my smoking friends to NOW, because of the low tar and nicotine benefits. After getting use to the hard draw, light flavor and falling ashes I was convinced that it was better for us to at least smoke low tar and nicotine cigarettes.  
Recently I have returned many cartons and packages to the store for replacement. WHAT IS THAT HORRIBLE SMELL?? I have heard all kinds of things, from MILDEW (that’s what it smells like) to a new process. Yesterday I saw the enclosed ad in the paper and realized you have lowered the tar & nicotine to 1.4! The carton I bought yesterday (it stinks) was the new NOW’s. Today I bought some old NOW’s (2 + 0.2) and they are O.K. It appears the new NOW’s stink. Will whatever smells so bad be harmful if I smoked it? Are you going to correct the smell? Or --- Should I convert to Carltons??? I can’t continue running back and forth to the store to exchange cigarettes. Most places are out of stock locally. I will probably continue with NOW’s if things get better.  

Respectfully,  
William C. Sloan  
William D. Sloan  
Camp Grayling, Grayling MI 49738
