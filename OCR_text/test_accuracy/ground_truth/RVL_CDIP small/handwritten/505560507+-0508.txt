Dear Sir:  
Since you sell cigarettes, why don't you sell just one kind of cigarettes if they're all the same? Since you know it's bad for your health, why don't you stop selling them? It causes emphysema, lung cancer, and heart diseases. If you want to know why I'm sending you this letter, it's because cigarettes are bad for your health. So please stop selling cigarettes!  

Yours truly,  
Annie Huard
