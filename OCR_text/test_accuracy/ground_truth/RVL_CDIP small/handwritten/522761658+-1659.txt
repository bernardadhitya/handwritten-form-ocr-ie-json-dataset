Sir,

I am writing to thank you for the coupons you have been sending me. But I do not like you telling me where I have to shop to take advantage of them. Since you do not know where I live it is not always possible or convenient for me to use the coupons for a special place.

Thank you for your kind attention to this letter. I appreciate the coupons and looking forward to more. I always buy the carton so don’t need pack coupons.

Thanks again,
I am,
