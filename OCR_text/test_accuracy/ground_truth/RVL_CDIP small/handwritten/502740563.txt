Analysis of TOP cigarette tobacco by Charles R. indicates a ethylene glycol monopropyl ether content of less than 35 ppm. Assuming a 10 g transfer rate to mainstream smoke and a daily cigarette consumption of 40 1 gram cigarettes, a single daily intake of ethylene glycol monopropyl ether would be 14 mg.

A typical outdoor worker inhales 10 m3 of air during an 8 hour day. Therefore at a airborne concentration of 100 mg/m3 (25 ppm), he would inhale 1060 mg of ethylene glycol monopropyl ether. This value is approximately 70,000 times higher than the estimated maximum dose from a 2 pack a day cigarette smoker.
