July 24, 1996

Dear Sirs at R.J. Reynolds Tabacco Co.,

Mr. Mark Conrad, I was not sure whom to direct my letter to, but since "CAMEL" has been my choice of cigarette for so many years, I decided to send it to you.

I am sure you are well aware of all the public knowledge concerning illness & death caused by smoking cigarettes.

I was diagnosed with throat cancer, had surgery to remove a tumor and went through 7 1/2 agonizing and debilitating weeks of radiation treatment. My family and I have been through a very emotional and physical rollercoaster because of this cancer. I have 2 small children under the age of five. I truly hope I will be here to see them grow up.

My doctors and I are most positive that the cause of my throat cancer is from smoking "CAMEL" cigarettes. If I had only known so many years ago what I know today, I never would have lit that first cigarette. Now we are all becoming quite aware of the true unforgivable hazards of smoking cigarettes.

I feel that $300,000.00 compensation for all the discomfort, pain and medical bills is well within reason. I am not asking for admission to guilt, and don't intend to take it any further if my request is met. If not, I'm prepared to file a suit against the R.J. Reynolds Tabacco Co. If lawyers become involved, the amount will be astronomical.

Sincerely,  
Jim E. Hill
