Dear Sirs;

I am a smoker of your cigarettes, up to now I have smoked in order - Winston, Salem, Vantage for years. Have enjoyed them the most. On a trip to Pa. I discovered a cigarette "NOW". I tried them and was very please with the taste & satisfied I will be a new smoker if only I could find who carries them. Very suprise to fine such a good taste and such low tar & nicotine. I would appreciate some information on this please.

Thank you very much
