The tobacco companies of the U.S.A.  
better wake up and organize; get their  
lobbyists and sales forces in line before  
"READ MY LIPS" gets the jump  
on them and Chapter 7 or 11 takes  
them over. I am 80 years old  
and have smoked cigarettes since  
I was 12 yrs. old. At the present  
I smoke NO Menthol ones, but  
looks like I will have fixed another  
habit, wild women, booze or dip snuff.  
When I walked in my tobacco shop  
this morning asked for 2 packs and  
laid down a five dollar bill ($5.00).  
I stood at the register waiting  
for my change. The cashier said  
"Just right." The cigarettes went to  
"250 per pack." (85 yrs. ago 2 for 25¢)  
The income has sure not gave up  
in that percentage.  

Maurice L. Smith Jr.
