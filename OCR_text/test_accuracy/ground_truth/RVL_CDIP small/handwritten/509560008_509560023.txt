ABSTRACT

QUERCETIN REVERSES TPA INHIBITION OF GAP JUNCTIONAL INTERCELLULAR COMMUNICATION IN RAT GLIAL CELLS

D.W. Bonbick and J.E. Trosko, Dept. of Pediatrics and Human Development, Michigan State U., E. Lansing, MI, 48824

The naturally occurring bioflavonoid quercetin reversed the TPA inhibition of gap junctional intercellular communication in rat glial cells. Fluorescent recovery after photobleaching (FRAP) analysis and a scrape loading/dye transfer assay demonstrated quercetin to reverse the TPA action in a dose and time dependent manner. Quercetin may reverse TPA inhibition of gap junctional intercellular communication by preventing TPA activation of protein kinase C. Quercetin can be used as a model compound to investigate the action of TPA-like chemicals on gap junction function in neural cells. (Research is supported by a grant from the R.J. Reynolds Company.)
