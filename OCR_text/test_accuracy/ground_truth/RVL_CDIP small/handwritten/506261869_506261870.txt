Project 574  
C.P. Box Full Print  
Actions taken on first production to reduce solvent: Reduced production rate to 85%  
Used additional oven  
Used maximum temperature  
Results - I immediately off cutter, retained solvent 70,000 mg. IR.  
Alternative to reduce solvent to 20,000 target:  
1. Use CP Box with 1/3 print  
2. Use aqueous lacquer  
3. Age printed material 3 weeks  
4. Use vacuum chamber  
5. Redesign drying system  
Alternative #2 is likely the best if questions of toxicity and compatibility can be answered satisfactorily. Alternative #4 and 3 give temporary solutions but would likely not be satisfactory for volume production. Alternative #1 unlikely to be accepted by Marketing.  
Cost  
Alternative 1 is less expensive. Alternative 2 will likely be the same. Alternate cost is minimal but too dependent on weather. Cost of Alternative 4 is approximately 17,000. Alternative 5 is costly as item of item 10.
