Congresswoman Anna Eshoo	May 13, 1998

Dear Anna,

I'm 76 years old, in excellent health and I’ve been smoking unfiltered cigarettes for 51 years. None of my eight children smoke, nor does my wife, nor would I ever encourage anyone to smoke, nor do I smoke in presence of non-smokers any more.

Hearing taxes upon taxes on cigarettes is outright unfair, however. Smoking never has and never will cause accidents, broken families and broken lives as do drugs and liquor. But drugs are illegal and not taxed and there aren’t any new taxes on liquors or cigars that are legal as are cigarettes.

Hopefully you’ll be reasonable and vote against more taxes on cigarettes. The price is already exorbitate.

You represent many people who are paranoid about smoke and prejudice against smokers. But you represent us smokers, too.

You’re always proclaiming people’s rights of choice. Please be reasonable and don’t tax people to death because their choice is not one you approve of.

Sincere best wishes  
Jim McLaughlin  
3313 Platden Dr.  
Belmont, CA 94002
