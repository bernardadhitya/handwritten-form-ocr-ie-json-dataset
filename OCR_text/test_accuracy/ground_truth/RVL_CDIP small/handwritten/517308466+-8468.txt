8-18-96

Hi;

My name is Tom Mahnens. I'm writing this letter to ask for a discount.

I work for General Motors. Me and eight of my fellow workers will have reached 30 yrs. with G.M. by the end of this year. I reached it this past February. Most of us have been together for at least 25 of those years.

While going through your new catalog (Fall '96) I saw a couple of items that I would like to get for us.

I would like to ask if I could get a discount if I purchase nine of any one item or both.

I am not sure of what to get yet. They are:
1. Khaki Camel Cap #20580 at $.9 each.
2. Cup O' Joe Coffee Cup #70020 at $4.50 each.

Most of us wear hats and/or drink coffee.

I have to be honest with you, not all of us are smokers, some cigars, but we all like the Joe Camel items.
