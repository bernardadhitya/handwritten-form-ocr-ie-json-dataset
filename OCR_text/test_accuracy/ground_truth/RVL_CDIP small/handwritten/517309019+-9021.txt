Dear Winston,

I have been attending the Nationals in Greenville for many years and I always took advantage of Winston Select samples and other promotions. I wrote what happened this year at the Nationals to you but never have gotten a response. Let me reiterate. Winston was giving out 2 packs of Selects but I wasn’t allowed to participate because I didn’t have my driver license on my person. I had other forms of I.D. (SS card, VFW membership, etc.) but the young lady refused me. I wasn’t about to go way back to the parking area where my license was in my glove box. The 3 other gentlemen I was with had their licenses and rec’d the cigarettes. Not only that but they have been receiving coupons & cigarettes & other promotions and I haven’t rec’d anything. I have been smoking Winston Select for the past 5 yrs. since I first went to the nationals. If I’m not a valued customer, so be it. I appreciated all
