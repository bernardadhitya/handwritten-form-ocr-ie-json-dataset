MARCH 30, 1983

TSB STATUS REPORT

I have assisted John Woods in selecting blends for use in screening studies and have participated in smoking and evaluating the screening prototypes.

My test L-19335 to make a Higher Nicotine 6-13-14 and a 6-13-14 without casing was completed in the Pilot Plant yesterday (3-29-83). Approximately 500 lbs. of each blend was prepared.

We have completed blending and cutting Eleven (11) special blends in our Blending and Cigarette Fabrication area. These blends ranged from 50 lbs. to 100 lbs.

Time Spent (1-1-83 to 3-30-83)

Blending and Cutting	45 hours
Developing and Evaluating Blends	10
Scheduling and Follow-up on tests	10
Total	65 hours

R.G. Shore
