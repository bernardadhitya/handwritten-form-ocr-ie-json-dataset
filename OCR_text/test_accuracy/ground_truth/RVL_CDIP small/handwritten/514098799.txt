12/30/88

R J Reynolds Co

Gentlemen:

Thanks for the coupon for a carton of Premier. I had 4 packs of them given to me. I tried very hard to smoke them, but after 65 years of smoking the packs of Lucky Strike every day for many years until Selens come along.

My Doctor told me that the menthol would surely kill me, at 83 years of age, I still smoke 2 packs of Selens regular every day and enjoy every puff. I spend more for cigarettes then I do for food.

As an old tobacco man raised on the farms, I think your chance is very small in promoting the most horrible tasting smoke ever.

I will admit it does my clothes by not dropping ashes on them.

Best wishes and thanks

J. W. Brady  
5815 Patton St  
St. Louis 63139 Mo
