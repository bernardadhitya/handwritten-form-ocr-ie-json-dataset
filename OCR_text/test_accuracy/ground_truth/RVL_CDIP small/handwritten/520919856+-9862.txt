SUMMARY OF WORK AT K-C (2/22/89 - 2/23/89)

APPROACHES

1. Experiment with different levels of CaCl2 on HP paper.
RESULTS: Highest level we tried was 8%. Lowest level was 2%. Highest level on outer wrap paper was better for reduction of sidestream irritation and aroma levels.

2. Experiment with a higher level of Calcium Acetate Hydrate.
RESULT: 6% level was worse than 2% for sidestream irritation and aroma levels.

3. Experiment with an even higher level of CaCl2 on HP paper.
RESULTS: The 12% CaCl2 was not noticeably different from 8% on sidestream irritation and aroma and was somewhat worse on taste. However, 12% CaCl2 was lower on sidestream irritation.

4. Experiment with an even higher porosity paper.
RESULTS: MG65 was better than HP (both treated with 8% CaCl2) for reduction of sidestream irritation and aroma. Main problem was reduced deliveries.

5. Experiment with 6.2k succ and 2% Calcium Acetate Hydrate on current outer wrap paper and different porosity inner wrap paper.
RESULT: Using the same outer wrap paper, the higher porosity inner wrap (2700 CARESTA) was better than the low porosity inner wrap (78063-5) for sidestream irritation and aroma reduction.
