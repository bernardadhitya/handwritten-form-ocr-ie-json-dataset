Information Copies To: T. L. Ogburn, T. J. Heiser

Mrs. Spaeh:

This is the copy of Georgia Pacific’s Weekly Newsletter Brunswick Week. Brunswick week is made readily available to some seven hundred employees.

It is a policy at this Georgia Pacific plant that they will not hire hourly workers whom smoke cigarettes.

The article on the back page of this issue clearly states smoking causes cancer and Georgia Pacific states it has been scientifically proven.

The smokers at this mill which are many and are very concerned if this is indeed a true statement made by Georgia Pacific.

Thank you
Concerned Smoker
