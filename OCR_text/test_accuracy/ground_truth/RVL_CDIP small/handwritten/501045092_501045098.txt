Subject	Monthly Report
Date	Oct 19, 1955
Job No.	RP-206

OBJECT:
To identify the acids and related compounds present in "C" Turkish tobacco.

SUMMARY:
Vanillic acid obtained as the methyl ester has been found in "C" Turkish tobacco. Last month there was reported the isolation of a dithio-rotatory compound believed to be the amide of either 6-hydroxy-β-glangforquin acid or β-hydroxy-β-glangf-5-oxoginic acid. The identity of the amide remains unsettled, but examination obtained this month indicates it to be α-hydroxy-β-glangforquinamide.

EXPERIMENTAL:
A. The isolation and identification of methyl vanillate.

1. Isolation by Chromatography
The ether soluble portions of fractions 32-36 and 37-39 from the original ammonlyzed fractions were combined and chromatographed on a cellulose column, 10 cm x 1.2 cm (diam). The material was eluted in a mother pot diluted with before, and added to the top of the column. The column was developed with before followed by: before - ether, ether, ether-methanol, and methanol. Fractions were collected in 40 ml. samples and after staining an infrared spectrum on the fraction slag were combined as follows:
