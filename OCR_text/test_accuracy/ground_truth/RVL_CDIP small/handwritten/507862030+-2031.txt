Isoamyl Isovalerate

Regulatory Status:
	• JEMA Gras (#2085)
	• Approved by FDA for food use (21 CFR 172.515)
	• Listed by the Council of Europe at a level of 60 ppm (except for chewing gum)

Toxicity Data:
Isoamyl Isovalerate is prepared by direct esterification of isoamyl alcohol with isovaleric acid under azeotropic conditions.

S. Circtander, Perfume and Flavor Chemicals (Aroma Chemicals), Vol. 1, no. 229, S. Circtander, Montclair, NJ (1969); cited by D.L.J. Opdyke, Food Cosmet. Toxicol. 7 89 (19 ).

Toxicity:
	Both the acute oral LD50 value in rats and the acute dermal LD50 value of isoamyl isovalerate in rabbits exceeded 5 g/kg.

O.M. Moreno, Report to RIFM, Aug. 2, 1976; cited by Opdyke, op. cit.
