Re: Brand Perspective Camel NTL Multi Pack & Carton Promotion Test

This provides the brand perspective regarding the results of the Camel NTL promotion. Although the final MD report has not been issued, the final results are in-house & were presented to Brand Promotion 10/25.

* Action Standards (not yet)

Objective
The objectives of this research are to:
Determine the level of competitive trial generated by:
The NTL pack promotion relative to the Camel pack promotional offer.
The NTL test carton promotion relative to the Camel carton promotion.

Measure the speed of product movement for:
The NTL tie-in test pack promotion relative to the Camel pack promotional offer.
The NTL tie-in test carton promotion relative to the Camel carton control carton promotion.

Determine the level of competitive conversion generated by the NTL tie-in test carton promotion relative to the Camel tie-in control carton promotion given a minimum level of competitive trial (i.e., 10%).

Key Findings
Pack Promotion - Competitive Trial / Product Movement
The NTL pack promotion in pack outlets was as effective in moving product to competitive smokers as the Camel promotion.
The NTL pack promotion in carton outlets was more effective in moving product to competitive smokers than the Camel promotion.

Carton Promotion - Competitive Trial / Product Movement
The NTL carton promotion was as effective in moving product to competitive smokers as the Camel promotion.
