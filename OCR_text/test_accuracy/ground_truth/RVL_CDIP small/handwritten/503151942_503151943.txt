Pacific Mountain Business Building Plan

Strategy
Implement a business building program behind CAMEL against 18-34 year old female smokers in the PMSA. Where Camel has its greatest vitality and has demonstrated an ability to attract female smokers.
The business building plan is incremental to CAMEL'S strong PMSA marketing activities target against 18-34 year old male smokers.

CAMEL	Share of Smokers	PMSA
18-24 Females	3.4
25-34 Females	2.8

Tactics
Expand CAMEL'S media in the PMSA to include 7 targeted and in-mass female monthlies, all with a white readership index greater than .165. Cost $1.6MM (23% of total media)

Reach females via target intercept sampling as part of the continuity field marketing program. No additional funds required.
