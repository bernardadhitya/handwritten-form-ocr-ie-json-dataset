Sirs - Ignoried "Letter to a Tobacco Co."
in Reader's Digest.

You make me sick. A short time ago, I read your full page statement in a magazine that tobacco smoking causes no disease etc.

For years I've wished there was some way I could see you for the death of my father. He smoked 2 packs of Camel a day for 50 years. He was a chain smoker in later years - caught in the addiction of nicotine.

Altogether, your cigarettes killed several of my relations too. For years I've been thru hell because of your cigarettes (the cigarettes I should say). I cannot take it, as I dare not breathe tobacco smoke. I am always out the door & test the air as to whether I can go in a building or stay in a room at the least smell of the smoke I get.
