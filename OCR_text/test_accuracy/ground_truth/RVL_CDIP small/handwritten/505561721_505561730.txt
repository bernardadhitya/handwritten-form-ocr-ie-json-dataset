Aug. 6, 1986  
R. J. Reynolds  
Tobacco Co.

Gentlemen:  
I am enclosing a (?) letter & have written to one of my Rep. Mike Synar. I thought you may be interested to know you have one man behind you.  

I did not give my Rep. much proof. I would like to give you that now.  

My father lived to be 94 years of age. He chewed tobacco all of his adult life, maybe even longer. I believe at least 80 years. He died of an heart attack. No proof that tobacco was the cause.
