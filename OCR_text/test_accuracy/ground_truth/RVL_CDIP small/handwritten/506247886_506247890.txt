Packaging Highlights	August 10, 1987

* Scalable innerliner:

- Three Archer structures including the unprinted overlap structure were re-evaluated on the innerliner machine. The unprinted overlap structure performed the best. Problems encountered on the first trial with these structures was attributed to machine problems.

- Twenty rolls of the Nopawp OPP/foil/Nopawp OPP structure produced by James River have been ordered to determine reproducibility of the structure and to be used for longer machine runs.

- Alpha product will be packed this week for package and shelf life evaluations. Package evaluations will be done on both Archer and James River OPP/foil/OPP structures.
