Smoke nicotine is controlled by 2 factors:
1) The amount of nicotine present in the tobacco rod
   (% tobacco nicotine x rod weight) and 2) how efficiently the nicotine is delivered (influenced by
   the dilution, filter efficiency, etc.).

From XGT studies, Camel Lt. 85 had a rod weight of 0.6695 g, tobacco nicotine of 1.73%. Thus 11.58 mg of
nicotine in the tobacco rod, smoke nicotine of 0.63 mg/cig for a % nicotine transfer of 5.44%.

If the Camel Lt. blend dbl. extra H2O with had the same tob. rod wt. as above, they only got a
% nicotine transfer of 3.87%.

Thus it is not possible that the tob. rod wt. was 0.4766 g which, at .27% blend nicotine and
5.44% nicotine transfer would give .07 mg nic/cig.

If this is the case and these cigs were made to firmness, the dbl. extra H2O blend would have greater filling capacity than
regular Camel Lt. 85-O blend.
