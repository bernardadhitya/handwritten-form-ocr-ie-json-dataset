4-27-88

Aroma Results

Summary:  Of the 24 products tested in the aroma study, changing the substrate flavor, tobacco jacket and paper flavor all appear to effect the aroma.  The short term change which needs consideration is the overwrap paper modifications, utilizing either the new 137 flavor, 104 without the Firmenich chocolate, 104 with reformulated Firmenich chocolate and the low pH paper.  Informal smoking results indicate the use of the 137 flavor is not desirable for smoking.

Short Term Next Steps:

Models have been made with 137 flavor, 104 without chocolate, and 104 with reformulated chocolate.  Models will be evaluated informally for taste.  Results will be available 4-29-88 and a recommendation will be made for changing paper flavor.

Cindi Davis will be conducting a disaster check for aging in the extreme environmental chambers.  Products will be evaluated visually and for taste.

The lower pH paper has gone through QDA testing.  Vivian Suh will be co-ordinating the results of those results through Ken Rush.
