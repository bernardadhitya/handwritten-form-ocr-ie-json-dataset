WEEKLY REPORT 3-28-85

Carbonwood filler - Work continues on the production of rolls of carbonized filler. The various steps for making the material were shown to Mike Eubie, Barry Fainting, and Millie Wong.

The two furnace (kilns) at the Dark power garage are completed and ready to operate. Since further samples of Sharp Prairie are not required from us we plan to test flux cored atoms these furnaces.

Sub Conrad and I are working on a design for a prototype of a continuous furnace for carbonizing flux cored atoms.
