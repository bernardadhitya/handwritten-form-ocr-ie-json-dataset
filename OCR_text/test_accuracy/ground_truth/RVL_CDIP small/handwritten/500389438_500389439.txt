Gentlemen:

I have tried your new cigarette "low" and I found that it was very difficult to smoke. I had to cut off about ¼ inch from the filter tip and found that the smoke came through a little more easily. However, the cigarette lasted about half the time of "Dual" which I have been smoking since they came on the market.
