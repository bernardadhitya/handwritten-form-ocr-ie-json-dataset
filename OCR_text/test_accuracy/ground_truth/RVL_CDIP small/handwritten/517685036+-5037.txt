To whom it may concern	12-18-95

I would appreciate it so much if you could send me another pack of the Seasons Greetings cigarettes. I am a collector and had them sitting on a table for everyone to look at during the holidays, but the problem is my nephew who isn’t two yet, while our backs were turned, put his hands on it; you can just imagine from there. So if you have an extra pack when you receive this letter I would appreciate another pack.

Deborah Titus
3503 Crestmore Cir.
Hsv. Al. 35816

Thanks so much
Happy holidays
Deborah J. Titus

P.S. My birthday is 3-29-75
