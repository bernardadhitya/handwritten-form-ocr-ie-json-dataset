Monthly Report #13  
Determination of Amino Acids in Tobacco  
Jan 19, 1954 thru Feb. 19, 1954  

Qualitative Determination of Amino Acids  
in Tobacco  

Qualitative determinations of the Amino Acids in the Flue-Cured Tobacco (R.J. 1953) during curing were continued and determinations of the Burley Tobacco were started.  
In review, one lb grass samples of each type Tobacco were extracted with 200 ml of distilled water plus Tobacco: Solute (1:4 w/w) Column Extraction Method(1). The Amino Acids in the extracts were then separated from the sugars, pigments, etc. with the use of Amberlite IR 120 (H+). See effluents from the ion exchange very concentrated. Made in column and qualitative determinations were done by Paper Chromatography.
