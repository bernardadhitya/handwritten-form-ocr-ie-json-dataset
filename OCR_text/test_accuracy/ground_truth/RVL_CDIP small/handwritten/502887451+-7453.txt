April 15, 1984  

Dear Sir or Ma’am,  

Yes, let’s separate fact from fiction if you’ve got the nerve to.  

Maybe, JUST MAYBE, cigarette smoking doesn’t cause cancer BUT I speak from experience in saying that the smoke from cigarettes, pipes & cigars MAKE ME SICK!!! My eyes itch, burn, water; I get a headache AND then I vomit!!! FACT — you people make things that make me sick & even prevent me from working or enjoying a social life where there is also smoking or one that stinks enough for fifty.  

You say non-smokers can be more tolerant. FACT — non-smokers have been tolerant for centuries!!!  

You say smokers and non-smokers have to talk to one another. FACT — the majority of cigarette smokers don’t give a damn about non-smokers. One actually wanted to see me get sick & the others made the comment “it’s my life”. IT’S MY LIFE ALSO DAMN IT!!!
