MBO  New Project

Measurement of Human Response to Tobacco Smoke

Objective:  To measure the human response to tobacco smoke as a more quantitative means of smoke satisfaction.

Planned Action:  The first step will be evaluation of electronic laboratory techniques such as the galvanic skin reflex to determine sensitivity. If a simple and reliable technique is established attempts will be made to correlate the human response to, among other things as, smoke satisfaction, mixture impact, smoke balance and flavor evaluation.
