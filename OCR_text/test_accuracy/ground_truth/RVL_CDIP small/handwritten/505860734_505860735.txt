Cigarette Smoke Condensate  
(1) (a-1) A II (a-1) A II and a 50:50 mixture of the two were evaluated on the full Alpha model. Results showed the (a-1) A II to have the most potential and the mixture next. The evaluation results showed that the levels were too high in regards to polar aroma and dry puff character and that the flavor only has potential as a part of the flavor system. See notebook page 364717 for evaluation results.  

(2) Quantitative and more refined qualitative research is continuing of (F-4) S-1 and (F-4) S-2 fractions. A phone conversation with Allister Smith resulted in a joint decision to discontinue analyses of the (F-4) S.R. portion because of low interest in it sensory contribution to smoke taste. See Henry & Chung January 22, 1986 report (attached).  

QDA  
QDA model No. 63 KWS showed promise as an improvement over previous models. Another lab model like No. 63 but using a new cleaner fuel source (No. 7085) has even more potential as an improved model and may be considered as a consumer test model.
