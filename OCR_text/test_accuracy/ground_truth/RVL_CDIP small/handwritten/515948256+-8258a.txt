To Whom it may concern:

I have been smoking Winston Select for about three years now, I have never had anything wrong with them till now! I got a carton from the store the other day like I always do! and took them home. I open them to find that only half of the were good, I mean that I could smoke at all, most of them had holes in the paper.

I tryed to take them back to the store were I got them but he would not take them because they were open, he told me he could not return them because of this, how do I know that there no good till I open them myself, I mean in N.Y. a carton of smokes cost me $25.00 more
