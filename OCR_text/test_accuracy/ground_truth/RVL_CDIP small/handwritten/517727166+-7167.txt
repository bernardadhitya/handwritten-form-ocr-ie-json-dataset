NO RESPONSE

July 14, 1992

To whom it may concern

When I get on T.V. and listen to the news it almost made me scream when they talk about smoking. I don’t smoke but I lost a lot of my family from cancer and they didn’t smoke or didn’t go around smokers and also I knew a lot of women that smoke that was pregnant and had normal children.

Sometimes food will give you cancer and if God meant you to die from cancer that’s the way you going to die.

C. B. C.

I am a resident of Fla
