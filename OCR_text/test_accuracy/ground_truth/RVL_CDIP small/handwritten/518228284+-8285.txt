4-25-96

Dear R. J. Reynolds,

I have been a loyal Monarch smoker for about 3 months now. Recently I purchased a carton of Monarch Light 100's. After smoking 3 of the packs, I noticed that one of the remaining had a small slit cut up the length of the pack. This made me very nervous because I was afraid that maybe this and other packs had been tampered with. Now, I'm not planning on giving up on the best low priced cigarettes on the market, I just thought that you should be aware of this unfortunate mishap.

I have enclosed a SASE in hopes for a response. Thank you.

Sincerely,
