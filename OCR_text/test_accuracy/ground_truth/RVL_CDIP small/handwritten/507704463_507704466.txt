to whom MAY Concern  
NOV 2 1990  

The people I work with at a factory I won't name at the present time been told that as of Dec 1, 1991, they must all quit smoking at least inside the plant.  
They can go out into the parking lot & smoke if they like, that's all. It has been a place where you could smoke at your worksite for 87 years now suddenly you have to quit. I know the Illinois clean air act has been passed to eliminate smoking in all workplaces except factories, factories, fabrooms, & poor halls & bingo places but is it
