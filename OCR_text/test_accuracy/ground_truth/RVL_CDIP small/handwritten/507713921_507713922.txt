WEEKLY A

SHAPING UP: U-Haul International in Phoenix, Ariz., is telling its employees to shape up or face a pay loss. Starting April 1, employees who are obese, anorexic or hooked on cigarettes will be docked 5¢ a pay check. Employees who get their weight under control and quit the smoking habit within two years will be rewarded with a refund.
