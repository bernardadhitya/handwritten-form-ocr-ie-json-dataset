SUMMARY OF PROPOSED WORK - (200 words or less): The maximal treadmill exercise responses of approximately 900 clinically normal males between 25 and 55 years of age will be studied to determine if there are any significant differences between cigarette smokers and non-smokers. The parameters to be compared between the two groups are:

1.	Duration of maximal exercise.
2.	Maximal heart rate.
3.	Systolic blood pressure during maximal exercise testing.
4.	Incidence of arrhythmias during maximal exercise testing.
5.	Differences in the computer quantitated changes in the ST segment slope and depression in response to maximal exercise.
