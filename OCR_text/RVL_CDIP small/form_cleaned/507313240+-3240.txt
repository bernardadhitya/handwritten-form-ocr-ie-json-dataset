COMMENTS:
This report updates results for Newport King only. Salem was not tested because of the change to new product. Reading the Salem Manufacturing product versus Newport shows the products rated at parity by the competitive smoker group. Attribute ratings, however, tend to favor Salem for less harshness, cooler, smoother, milder, more menthol taste, menthol tobacco balance, fresh aftertaste, less drying and strong aftertaste, more sweet and cooling aftertaste, and less bitter taste. Franchise smokers continue to rate Salem superior to Newport. Results will not be issued to Brand you this wave.
