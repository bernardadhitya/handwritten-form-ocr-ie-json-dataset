Project No:	2166
Study Director:	TME
Date of Commission:	05-MAY-88
Short Title:	
Title:	MUTAGENICITY OF THE MAINSTREAM AND SIDESTREAM WHOLE SMOKE CONDENSATES OF THE TEST CIGARETTES ORION-97, -99, -101, -103, AND -105 IN THE SALMONELLA TYPHIMURIUM STRAINS TA98 AND TA100

Status:	Final Report

Objective:	Investigation of the mutagenicity of the mainstream and sidestream smoke condensates of 5 test cigarettes with various filter densities (range: 195 to 295 g/ft) in the Salmonella reverse mutation assay using strains TA98 and TA100.

Main Results:	Statistically significant increases in the specific mutagenicity with increasing filter density of the test cigarettes in strain TA98 and for sidestream smoke condensate in both tester strains.

Project No:	3140
Study Director:	TME
Date of Commission:	25-JUL-88
Short Title:	AMES, NITRATE CIG.
Title:	MUTAGENICITY OF THE MAINSTREAM AND SIDESTREAM WHOLE SMOKE CONDENSATES OF THE TEST CIGARETTES 11, 12, 14, AND 13 AND THE STANDARD REFERENCE CIGARETTE 2R1 AS DETERMINED IN THE PLATE INCORPORATION ASSAY USING THE SALMONELLA TYPHIMURIUM STRAINS TA98 AND TA100

Status:	Final Report

Objective:	Investigation of the mutagenicity of the mainstream and sidestream smoke condensates of 9 test cigarettes with various nitrate concentrations and filter in the plate incorporation version of the Salmonella reverse mutation assay using strains TA98 and TA100.

Main Results:	Statistically significant increases in the specific mutagenicity with increasing nitrate concentration in the filter of the test cigarettes were obtained for mainstream as well as for sidestream smoke condensate in strain TA98 in the presence of a metabolic promutagen activation system.
