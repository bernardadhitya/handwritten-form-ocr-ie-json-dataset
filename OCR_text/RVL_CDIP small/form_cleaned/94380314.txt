EFFECTIVENESS OF Pre-Sell – Harley Transition Booklet/Coupon – Maverick/Harley BIG1F  
(Report on December 13 only)  
PRE-SELL:  Obviously, even pre-sell campaign was not effective – most accounts received what they ordered. Retail accounts showed no buy back cause had 1 program as cost part received their Maverick two weeks after than the projected date.  

HARLEY TRANSITION BOOKLET/COUPON:  The booklet/coupon was fantastic. It alerted the consumer to the upcoming change over – because of the coupon, consumers anxiously anticipated the arrival of Mavericks – they were definitely ready to come twice coupons!  

MAVERICK/HARLEY BIG1F:  This was an extremely effective program. It changed the perception of cells and gave our introductory gift packs on Mavericks. Somewhat enabled us to produce our sales as full visual facing ran out while we still had Mavericks in sights package on the distribution warehouse.
