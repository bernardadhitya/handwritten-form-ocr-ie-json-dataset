PARK 500  
QUALITY ACCEPTANCE FORM  
ALLIED CLARIFLOC C-331 POLYMER  

Objective  
Determine the properties of CLARIFLOC C-331 polymer each time a shipment arrives. Upon arrival of the shipment, a bottom sample and top sample will be collected from the tank truck and the Waste Treatment Specialist personnel will perform the analysis.  

Procedure  
1. Have the driver collect 600 ml or more of sample from the top of the tank truck and from the bottom of the tank truck.  
2. Check the two samples for odor, color, trash, temperature, and viscosity. NOTE: for viscosities greater than 40,000 cps, use the #4 spindle on the 6 rpm setting and multiply the viscometer reading by 700; then return the spindle rpm setting back to 12 rpm for future tests.  
3. Notify the supervisor in charge whether the shipment is accepted or rejected as soon as possible.  
4. Discard the sample.  
5. File Acceptance Form in Lab drawer file.  
6. Attach to this form, any certificate of analysis that may be supplied by the vendor.
