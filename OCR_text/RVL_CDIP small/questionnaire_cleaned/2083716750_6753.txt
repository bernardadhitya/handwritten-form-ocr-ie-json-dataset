Marlboro Bar Program (A)  
Sweepstakes / MSA Questions  

1. What are the first two things you ask a consumer when you approach him / her during a promotion?  
Are you a smoker? Are you over 21?

Address  
DOB

21+

1. Door  
2. Kiosk

No

Yes

6 days 4 nights
