DEAR R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Co,
I am writing you this letter after reading your two page advertisement in the Feb 4-10 T.V. guide. (Please find enclosed).
Sirs, this "issue" about smoking that you claim is so "complex" is very simple to me.
I have been smoking cigarettes for twenty two years, I feel that this qualifies me as an expert on the subject of smoking. Not only is smoking very dangerous and damaging to your health, it also stops a person from having a normal life.
I first began smoking at age fifteen in bathrooms at high school because it was advertised to be the "cool" thing to do and

Feb 9 1984

Note 2nd the hell out the smoking ads. Quit while you can.
