Jan. 21, 96

The Camel V.I.P. Club:

I am writing to thank you for my Doc Camel lighter. Did you ever send out free samples? I'll take those too!

Ieshatchin really writing to tell you is that these non-smoking nuts are really going overboard. I've enclosed a letter from one of them who ate a letter from one. I don't know how they ever got me on these lists. I answered their survey & sent it back. They won't show that on around. I got them my Smoker's Rights decal also.

Give I'm a fed for 35 yrs, Camel's Non-Filters. From the time that I got out of school until I retired I worked in bars. At the end of the night all you could see on the band was their feet. That's how Smokey it was in some of the places I worked in. My heart is fine, blood pressure O.K. according to my doctor. Recent check-ups -
