Cavity Filter Additions

Added small amount of sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3), sodium carbonate (Na2CO3) and ascorbic acid to the tobacco end of the filter.

Subjective evaluation of all cigarettes show negative perception attributes on smoke flavor. NaHCO3 & Na2CO3 impart salty taste to last few puffs. Ascorbic acid gives a bitter acidic taste to the puff when drawn at large puff volume.

At small puff volumes, NaHCO3 addition seems qualitatively acceptable and may have a subjective advantage. Samples were submitted for aldehyde & HCN analysis as well as TPM/Nicotine.

control Winston	TPM 15.5 ± 3	Nic 93 ± .05
