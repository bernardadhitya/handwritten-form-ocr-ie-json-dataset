Nov. 14, 1997

R. J. Reynolds Co.  
Over three (3) months ago, I mailed in for a coupon application to send seventy (70) total seals for a free carton of cigarettes. I have never received one!!!  

Your company is still advertising the free cartons, but yet I’m still waiting to receive the one I requested months ago. Why?  

Why do you advertise these types of things, then never follow through?!!  

I smoked & supported the R. J. Reynolds Co. for thirty-two (32) years. I was a faithful user of your products all these years, until heart & lung disease ended my tobacco habit.  

I also have at least twenty (20), or more, family members in my immediate family that are still smoking. There are four (4) smokers living in my home!  

I fully realize that the Tobacco Co’s are going thru a lot of legal and financial problems, but, so is the consumer!!!! Just as you, we could use a helping hand!  

I’m faithful to buy your products, the least your company could do, is back up its ad campaigns!!!  

I’m still waiting to send for my free carton of cigarettes, hope to hear from Reynolds soon.  

Sincerely  
Margaret Dulan
