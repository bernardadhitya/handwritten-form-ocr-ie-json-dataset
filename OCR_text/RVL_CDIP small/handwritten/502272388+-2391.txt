3-26-83

Dear Miss

I read the write up in The York Dispatch where your Co R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Co was in a hazard over smoking cig.

I think this sort of thing has gone to far against the tobacco trade. I saw them on a farm in North Carolina near Fuquay Springs.

I am 66 years old and in good health and I still hold a steady job and I started smoking when I was 14 years old. I smoked R.J.R. that at this time came in a cloth bag you had to roll your own.

I did this for several years until I started smoking ready made cig.

I smoked 2 or 3 pks a day from then until about a year ago & dropped back to two very fast

MAR 02 1984
