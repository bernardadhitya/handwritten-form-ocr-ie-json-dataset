Act 1

Dear Sirs,

I've been wanting to write you for some time now and thanks for giving me a chance to voice my opinion.

I have been smoking for 60 of my 76 years and have had no problems. I smoke 3 packs 2 cartons a day. I was born a blue baby and the doctor told my mother I wouldn't see my 5th birthday. I had heart trouble into my teens but when I got married at age 23 the doctor could no longer find any heart problem. I was one of 6 daughters, no brothers, 3 of whom are now dead. The three who didn't smoke are the ones who died. The three who did smoke are still living. My oldest sister died of the flu, the second of heart trouble and a younger sister died of cancer of the throat. None of whom ever smoked.
