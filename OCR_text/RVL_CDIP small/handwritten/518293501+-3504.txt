To Whom it Concerns -

I was smoking a Doral light and a piece of ash fell on to my blouse and when I pulled the hot ash off - I found a piece of strange material in it - the cigarette had more of it - some is encased in green saran wrap - besides burning a hole in my blouse and scorching me It was a strange thing to be smoking - what wasn’t? Smokers get pluck enough is there truth to additives?
