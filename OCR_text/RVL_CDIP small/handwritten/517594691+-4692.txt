9/17/86	Possible Highlight	Pat Perfetti  
Study of Indoor Air Quality in New York City  

Under the auspices of the Tobacco Institute, plans have been made to conduct an indoor air quality study with the briefcase sampling systems in restaurants and offices in New York City during the week of 22 September.  
With the assistance of Mr. Mike Curry, Public Affairs, RJRT, sampling sites in fourteen different office buildings have been arranged. Dr. Guy Oldaker and Ms. Patricia Perfetti assisted in site selection in six of the buildings on 12 September and met with representatives of the Riese Organization, (a company owning the most restaurants in New York City) to arrange for sampling in restaurants.  
The level of carbon monoxide, nicotine, and respirable suspended particulate matter will be measured at each location. Sampling will be conducted by Dr. Oldaker and Mr. Fred Conrad, RJRT, Dr. Robert Fenner, Phillip Morris, Dr. William Crouse, Lorillard, and four representatives of ACVA Atlantic Incorporated.  

It is noted that the information collected in this study and the data used to support the conclusions are to be held in strict confidence by qualified persons only.
