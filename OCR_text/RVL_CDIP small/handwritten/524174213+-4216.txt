Thursday	April 8, 1999
Dear R.J. Reynolds,
I don’t have a touch tone phone and I would like to be on your smoking test. I am 36 years old. I am a white female. I smoke 100’s or regular menthol cigarettes. Either Kool’s or Salem’s I buy cigarettes by the carton. But now am starting to buy an extra pack here or there. I smoke at breaks in work or after work maybe at lunch. I don’t smoke when I drive. Most of places such as my work has a no smoking policy. People-workers are made to smoke outside. We used to have smoking in the building. I also smoke Newport 100’s. Most of my friends deny their habits. They say “no thanks I don’t smoke.” I don’t know what got into them (or they polite) “no! I don’t smoke.” That’s a fact. My grandmother had emphysema and smoked Winston. She died 20 years ago. God rest her soul.
