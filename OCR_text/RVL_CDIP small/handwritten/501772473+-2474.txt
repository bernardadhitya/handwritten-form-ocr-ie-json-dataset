Altus, Oklahoma.
410 North Trady Street.
July 16th, 1954.

Brg. Reynolds Tobacco Co.,
Winstisalem, North Carolina.

Dear Sirs,

I see in newspapers, of cigarette smokers claiming cigarette smoking caused cancer of their lungs and have sued some Tobacco Companies for damages; I do not believe any suits will be decided. I favor of the cigarette smokers because of the fact medical science does not as yet know the real cause of cancer, but in my case of irritated lungs caused by having smoked Camel cigarettes can easily be proven and I had an X-ray picture made of my chest in 1946 that will back up any other proof that smoking Camel cigarettes or some ingredient in the smoke therefrom irritated both my lungs to the extent I have been unable, and I will continue to be unable to get any relief from, this irritation has been of damage to my life and health, wife and son to an amount beyond me being able to estimate in dollars.
