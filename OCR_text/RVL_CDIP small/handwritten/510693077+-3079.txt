Abiotic diseases
non-pathogenic, usually not lethal

Fertilizer-salts injury
1) roots too close to fertilizer
2) dry soil conditions
3) most severe in sandy soils

should be 3"-5" away from roots
use combination fertilizer-transplant
procedure to maintain relative position
apply single hand deep below root but
	not really consistent
	also, leaching makes it unavailable
bad to broadcast fertilizer, but makes
	injury more likely if other conditions
	prevail
economy of broadcast is poor	||||| 3" min side
transplanting without fertilizer is a
	growing practice

Irregularity in size with f-s injury
find by digging it up - can find fine band
	roots "latch up"
doesn't matter if fertilizer is liquid or powder

Irrigation immediately after transplant
	helps eliminate f-s injury

Wireworm injury
more prevalent in Coastal Plain than Piedmont
goes in right around soil line, tunnels
use insecticide - see Sol. Info
