Dear Joe,

On October 29th I sent you 510 C-notes along with a catalog order for a Color Watch, Tennis shoes, & Tote Bag.

As of yet I have not received them and am wondering if my order was received by you, or perhaps?

If there is a problem at your end then I shall remain patient, for I've always received great service from you in the past.

I mailed the C-notes in a return addressed, sealed manila envelope so it seems very unlikely that it could have been overlooked.

My neighbor had a problem with U.P.S. a while back, so maybe that's the answer. It's just that it took me quite awhile to collect all those C-notes & I hate to think it was all for nothing. If you have any information, I would very much appreciate hearing from you.

Thank you very much for your attention;
