Dear R.J. Reynolds-  
My husband & I have smoked your MORE cigarettes since 1975. We do not want to have that smoking other brand. I smoke the green menthol, he smokes non-menthol-red.  

I have written you many times with the same complaint. I do not tie you again because the problem has become ridiculous. The last 3 cartons of Rio non-menthol & the last 3 cartons of my menthol, the cigarettes will not stay lit. I am not exaggerating when I say all have to light & relight 1 cigarette 10 times or more. Can you imagine how frustrating this is, what a waste of time it is, what a waste of lighter. Imagine trying to leave with a cigarette - you go 2 feet with a cigarette ex out. Stop the madness, fix the issue & relight it, come back out, restart the match, makes 2 feet cigarette out.  

This makes no sense so angry, cost keeps happening over & over. We pay good money for these cigarettes & do not deserve this aggravation. The price of your cigarettes has gone up 4 digits. It wastes a lot of my good money. We each smoke a carton a week which is 64.00. There is $85.00 per for good. You do the figures. It is approaching the point where we can no longer afford them.  

I think we at least deserve a top quality product. Furthermore, we are disappointed that R.J. Reynolds ignores its faithful customers. We used to receive coupons merchandise offers, lovely Xmas ornaments, Asa Shaw Jr. We no longer receive any of this but we do receive masses of groups of coupons every week for other cigarettes - very generous.
