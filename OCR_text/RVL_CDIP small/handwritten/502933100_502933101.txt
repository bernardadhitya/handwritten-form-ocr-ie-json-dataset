4/26/84

Dear R.J. Reynolds,

Regarding your recent advertisement in Time Magazine 4/30/84: Who are you people trying to kid? When is a tobacco company actually going to admit that there is anything wrong with cigarettes. As far as the tobacco companies are concerned, cigarettes don’t cause cancer in smokers. You continue to present so called authorities who maintain there is no conclusive evidence that cigarettes cause lung cancer. And now in light of some of your recent set backs in smoker vs non smoker rights, (i.e. San Francisco) you are running a campaign to educate or is it manipulate the public. By the way, it’s ironic that the tobacco industry with its huge Government Subsidies and supposed hard times can still manage to spend millions in lobbying efforts.

Any way as far as I’m concerned you’ll maintain that the jury is still out on anything linking smoking and cancer. I just wanted to inform you of some things the jury isn’t still out on.

To non smokers, cigarette smoke is foul smelling, and annoying. There is no question that the retinch from as you euphemistically call it, “passive” smoke permeates clothing, or irritates eyes and throats.
