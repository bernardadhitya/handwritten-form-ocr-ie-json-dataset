April 9, 1997

Dear Mr. Goldstone  
Chairman / CEO  
RTR Nalisco Hldgs

The Politicians and the Media / attorneys for anti-smoking have painted a picture of the Tobacco Industry as the cause of all the problems and ills in Society. I hope the Industry continues to fight in the courts for a worthy cause – protection of capitalism and human rights. The real fear should be “liberalism.”  
I personally believe, as everyone else with any common sense, that the whole issue revolves around money and politics. In that realm the Tobacco Industry should strike a deal with the politicians / attorneys etc.

A. Tobacco Industry – Agrees To:  
1. Assist in the prevention of Teens / underage smokers. Modify Advertisements.  
2. Place a label on cigarette packs – “For adult consumption only.”  
3. Continue R & D for safer cigarette products.  
4. Increase price of a pack of cigarettes by 5¢. The funds to be placed in a special fund for Cancer Research of all Types. To the benefit of all

A LEGAL PRODUCT
