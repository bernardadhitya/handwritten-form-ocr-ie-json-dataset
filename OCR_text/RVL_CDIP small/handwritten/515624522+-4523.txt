Sept. 16, 1997  

Dear Mr. Gladstone:  

I am writing to let you know that I, along with my Dominican Congregation, are participating in INFact’s boycott of RJR Nabisco products.  

To endanger children and young people with products of tobacco which can destroy them is not acceptable to me.  

I am also educating many others because I am a teacher who influences many - parents and children; I also come from blood family that is large and believes in healthy children.  

Please have the courage to confront this problem. Thank you.  
Emma Kielbanek, G.P.
