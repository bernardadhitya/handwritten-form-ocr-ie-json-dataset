Quality Control  

Gentlemen:  

I smoke a carton weekly and Feud is my brand. Last week as I started my weekly travel job I as usual bought a carton. Almost every cigarette in the carton was less than satisfactory. The cigarette fell neatly off the filter either in the pack or dropped from my lip. Fortunately I did not burn my clothes or car upholstery. Thought you would want to know.  

Sincerely  
Joe B. Furr
