To whom it may concern, Jan. 6th 1979

I have been buying Sebrings as long as they come on the market, but on Jan. 5th, 1979 I found soil inside the tobacco of just about a whole pack and they wouldn't stay lite so I had to throw them away. Am writing to let you know this, so maybe you can do something about it.

Thank you!
Susan Blue

Baldwin, N.Y. 14715
