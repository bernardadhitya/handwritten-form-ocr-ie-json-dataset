Dear Customer Representative,  

Winston Light 100’s are my favorite cigarettes and I have been smoking them for many years. When I smoke a cigarette, I smoke all of it to the tobacco end. Over the last several months, I have had a problem with some of the cigarettes. I always buy my cigarettes by the carton and the problem seems to be certain cigarettes within the carton. Also, not every carton is affected with the problem. The problem I am speaking of is this: I will be smoking and almost to the end, approximately one eighth of tobacco left. For some reason, the remaining tobacco falls out of the cigarette of its own accord. I have ruined several pieces of clothing, a cushion, and a sofa because of this problem. I am very careful now when I smoke to be sure the cigarette is over an ashtray toward the end. I felt sure you would want to know about this problem because after smoking Winston Lights 100’s for years with no problems, and now after almost three or four months with the problem I am hoping it can be fixed.  

Sincerely,  
Betty Brown
