On 4/5/98, I purchased (2) two single packs of Total Light 100s menthol at Eckerd Drugs.

They were kept behind the counters and clerk required to get them. Both packs had the filters removed from them as they sat on the shelf. When I asked what had been done to them I was told he didn’t know—because all the packs had the items removed.

Being kept out of customer reach, this had to have been done by the store.
