FLAVOR STATUS	A. Gonzalez
4-23-80

1. PTGA-142 (10% alc) was evaluated at two different levels, 1% and .5%. The flavor was evaluated on substrate containing 7.5% CAG, 8% GPA and 20% B3. In both instances PTGA-142 was perceived as improving the smoke taste of alpha although a pronounced offtaste was detected.
Recommendation: Reduce the level of (A-1) fraction present on PTGA-142

2. Precursor Mix #59 has been applied to the fiber glass and will be evaluated next week.

3. Various solid natural extracts are being evaluated on the substrate, some of these extracts are: Gentian root, valerian root, Juniper Berry and Guarana seed.
Evaluation results will be available next week.
