Object
Development of a feasible Sutton-type tobacco substitute which has no undesirable biological activity.

Summary
A. Production of Sutton Filler
Approximately 250 lbs. of Sutton filler has been prepared to date for test lots. The quantity of paper to size each week has been increased from 14.4 kg to 16.0 kg. The use of nitrogen dioxide in 3,000 lbs. of nitrogen steel of 150 lb. cellulose has effected a large reduction in the color of the nitrogen dioxide and has reduced the time required to mix the nitrogen dioxide and paper from 20 hr. to 8 hr. per week.

B. Fabrication of Cigarettes
