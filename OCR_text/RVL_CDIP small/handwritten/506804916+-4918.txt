Volatile Aromatic Hydrocarbons (VAH)

References: F. W. Best, T. S. Silk, R4DM, 1975 No 59

Experimental conditions:
Smoking Equipment	Multi-port/piston Filamentii-based smoking machine
Sidestream Enclosure	"Harris total smoke collection chamber"
Sidestream Flow Rate	1.25 L P M
Cigarette per sample	1
Lighting procedure	Electric preheat, contact 5 sec
Component Trapping System	Cambridge pad (44 mm)
	followed by drying tube tee (250 mg)
	followed by charcoal coconut base tube (200/400 G.C.C.)
Sample Work-up	Front and back sorbent sections placed in separate GC vials. 1 mL of CS2 was added to each vial. 150 µL of n-octane (1%) is also added to each vial. Each vial was then capped immediately. The vials were then shaken for at least 30 min.
