Dear Sir or Madam:  
I would like to tell you that Joe Camel has made a big impact on children, and that impact is a bad one. Your message is saying to kids that smoking is cool, smoking is not cool. If your children were to smoke because of a silly cartoon character, would you not be mad angry at the company who made up this character? Joe Camel is really not cool if he smokes. Your company may promote messages to kids that smoking is not cool, but that does not compete with Joe Camel. If you want to promote Camel cigarettes than adults not cartoon characters. Cartoon characters appeal to children not adults. If you want adults to use your product than use adults for your ads.
