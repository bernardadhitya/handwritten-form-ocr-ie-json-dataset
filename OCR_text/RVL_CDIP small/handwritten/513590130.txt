P.J. Hoult		July 8, 1989
Dear Mr. Von Arx,
I am writing to let you know that I, and thousands of other women, am deeply offended by the new "updated" CAMEL advertising.
These characters, plastered on billboards all across the country, are deliberate representations of male dominance in its most repugnant form. We will not tolerate it, and hold you personally responsible for removal.
