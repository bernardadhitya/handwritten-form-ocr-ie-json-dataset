General Research Areas of Interest to RJR

1. Raw Material - Nature of these materials, changes taking place in the production of these materials and the influence of these changes on quality etc., the efficiency with which these materials are processed and utilized.

2. Why Do People Smoke &/or What Are Characteristics of Satisfying Smoke
   What if any flavor attributes can a smoker assign to a cigarette and its smoke.
   How accurately can the public on a panelist judge tar and/or nicotine levels, strength etc.
   How does public perceive tar & nicotine relative to smoking-health issues (equally "hazardous" (?)) and what levels would be regarded as "safe."
