To Whom It May Concern

I am writing this letter to express my extreme concern and displeasure every time I see one of your repulsive and disgusting billboards. Are you promoting cigarette smoking and bestiality?! Smoking is bad enough but dressing a camel in a tux and putting a lewd look on his face with a sexy woman in the background - you're despicable!

I will do my best to tell everyone I know or don't know to take a look and see what you're trying to pull. The fight is on.

RECEIVED
SEP 20 1989

Barbara Zingale
