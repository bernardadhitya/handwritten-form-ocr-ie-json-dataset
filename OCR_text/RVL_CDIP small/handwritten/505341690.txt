Subject	Philip Morris entomology research group.
To	Alan Rodgman
Date	1-17-93
From	Herman J. Benzel

The P.M. group presented 2 papers at the Tobacco Workers Conference. Both are associated with the use of methoprene: 1) A large-scale evaluation of habitat for control of Diadromus pulchellus (?), and 2) Comparison of larval feeding in different types of tobacco by the cigarette beetle. The second reported giant larvae when feeding on methoprene treated tobacco. This is in agreement with my research data which was also presented at this meeting. A study involved with a pheromone to monitor cigarette beetle was scheduled for presentation by entomologists at P.M. This paper, however, was not approved for presentation.

P.M. has hired a new female research technician. The group now consists of J.T. Long (group leader), D. Faustini, R.W. Lehman, M.T. Minor and the new technician M.A. Mangelli, who was the previous leader, is now responsible for entomology at the international level.

From discussions with Dr. Mangelli it seems that they are aware of the lethal effects of methoprene on the second generation of doses below 1.0 ppm. It will also be sending me information on the entomology advisory committee of TAUS.
