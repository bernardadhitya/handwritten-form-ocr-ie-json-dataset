Dear Sir,  
My husband has enjoyed your cigarette for years, but recently bought some & had troubles. He couldn't draw any smoke from the cigarette. He used a needle & poked a hole in the top, & still it didn't work. He bought some more & they were ok. Maybe this one batch was bad.  
I am sending you one of the pkg. with code number.  
Thank you for your time.  

Mrs. Lois Kaperki  
830 Bond Ave.  
Collinsville, Ill. 62234
