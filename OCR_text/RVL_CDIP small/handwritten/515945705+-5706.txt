To Whom it may concern:  

I have been smoking Camel cigarettes for 41 years. I smoked Non Filter until you started putting filters on Camels and I changed to Filter Camel and later to Filter Camel 100’s. My complaint is this: You have never given me a rebate of any kind except (1) one time you sent a coupon good for 25¢ off a carton of cigarettes. I collected over 2000 & notes and mailed them to you for merchandise that I never got nor did I get credit for the 2000 notes. My wife Mary smokes Desert 100’s full flavor; she sent you over 500 proof of purchase coupons and asked for merchandise. She never got the merchandise nor the credits either. As to make a long story short, we are looking for another brand of cigarettes that stands behind their promotions and has cigarettes of good taste, also who appreciates their customers that they will send out coupons for reduced prices on cigarettes. Do to close, don’t send us any more surveys to fill out (3) three each in the past year and if you treat all your customers this way, I do wonder you are in business.  

Sincerely  
James A. Sheffield  
3-10-98
