Dear friends

Just a few lines to let you know what some are doing. Sister Pat has had put into paper details and wants to charge $1 for paper. The leg. for children didn’t have to pay. She wants that for his self. I believe all he is doing is to get money out of the C. of J. to give him and not only money. I talked to some people and if they don’t want to come there and they are going to come from here I wish they would go ahead and see. On Saturday night they was only about all the reason he is about playing and trying to play whore here. Has been trying to get another moment in to people job. He is not qualified for Preacher. I say you all consider this. I’m in a hurry. I have got to get away at fast post. Would hate to see this let longer. Fire
