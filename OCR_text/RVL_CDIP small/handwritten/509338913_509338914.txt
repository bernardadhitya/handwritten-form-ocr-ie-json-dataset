Comments on the Winston 85 memo AR to RDM

I found Alan’s comments very interesting, but I disagree with him that the 1978 Winston resembles the current 1982 Winston; many changes have occurred. E.g.

Ventilation (~20%) changes which would affect his statements on TTI pH, and general delivery of flavor components.

Tobacco inventories available all generally higher in 1982 than 1978. These leaves used in 1977-78, which would affect overall flavor and TTI/etc.

Blend additives such as the level of sugar and casings - higher in the 1982 Winston which could affect TTI again and flavor stability such as harshness, moistness and strength. And finally,

QTF Variable inclusion in the Winston

The franchise demographics e.g. 25-34 yr old (1978) → 18-34 yr males secondary franchise (1982), M to F ratios lower in 1978 to present, etc.

Mailboxes have also been changed and as “normal” we have been trying as always to copy.

Alan’s views are somewhat static, single look in history while in fact the FF generic cigarette category was a dynamic system where changes occur monthly if not daily based on changes on the major brands, faded the smoker ideals and wants for best delivery.
