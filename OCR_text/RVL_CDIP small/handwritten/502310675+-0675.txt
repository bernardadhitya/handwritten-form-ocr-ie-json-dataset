To Whom It May Concern:  
I have been smoking Vantage Ultra Light 100's for some time now & really enjoy these cigarettes. A couple of weeks ago I purchased a carton of Vantage Ultra Light 100's from an A&P Grocery Store in Alexandria La. Every pack in that carton had cigarettes with defects in them, at least four defects to a pack & I thought you'd be interested in knowing of this defect.  

The paper that goes around the filter was shortened & did not meet covering the air flow not to get through these three cigarettes had to be thrown away. Because I do not care for unfiltered cigarettes.  

I have been smoking for around 15 years and was delighted to discover just what I wanted in a filtered 100 cigarette in Vantage Ultra Light 100's. Previously I had smoked menthols & I enjoy smoking as it helps to me relieve stress, which I have more than my share of, with a husband who has lymphoma cancer & is hospitalized a great deal of the time. Again I thought you'd be interested in this matter.  

Sincerely
